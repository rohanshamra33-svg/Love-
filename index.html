Our Story ❤️

It still feels a little unreal that it's been a year and a half of us. I remember being nervous about the smallest things in the beginning, and now you're the person I text the second something funny happens, just because I know you'll laugh with me before anyone else does.

You've turned so many ordinary days into ones I actually remember — dumb jokes that somehow became our jokes, arguments that ended in laughing at how silly we were being, quiet moments where we didn't even need to talk and it still felt like enough.

Eighteen months went by faster than I expected, and somewhere in there you became one of the most important parts of my life without either of us really announcing it. It just happened, the way the right things do.

I'm genuinely excited for everything we haven't done yet — the memories we haven't made, the inside jokes we haven't invented, the future versions of us I keep imagining. This is just the beginning, and I'm so glad I get to do it with you.

A Letter I Want You To Keep Forever

My Anshu, I don't really know how to start this without sounding like every other love letter ever written, so I'll just say it plainly: these past 1.5 years with you have been some of the best of my life. You showed up and made things easier without even trying to. The bad days got softer because I knew I could talk to you about them. The good days got better because you were the first person I wanted to share them with. Somewhere between the long calls, the random memes, the small fights and the even smaller apologies, you became home to me. I think about the little things the most — the way you laugh at your own jokes before you even finish telling them, the way you get quietly determined about things you care about, the way you show up for me even when you're tired or having a rough day yourself. I notice all of it, and I'm grateful for all of it. Thank you for staying beside me, for choosing me on the easy days and the hard ones, for being patient with me while I figure things out. I don't say it enough, but I see how much you give to this, and to me, and I don't take that for granted. I want more of this. More stupid inside jokes, more late night conversations, more memories that don't mean much to anyone but us. I want to keep growing with you, keep learning you, keep choosing you — today, next year, and every year after that. So here's to 1.5 years, and to everything we still get to build together.
Forever yours,
Rohan ❤️
